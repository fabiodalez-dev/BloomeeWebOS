# Bloomee on LG

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
The existing BloomeeTV music player is being adapted for the owner's rooted LG 43UN70006LA running webOS 5 with a remote control. The owner listens to a personal Jellyfin music library from a sofa and explicitly wants music to continue with the physical screen switched off after a configurable idle interval.

## Existing identity
Preserve the source project's dark plum surfaces, pink accent, album artwork and track list hierarchy. Use the existing album screenshot in assets/docs/tv/album.jpg as the visual reference. The interface is a music player rather than a marketing page.

## Constraints and principles
Readable large controls and a visible remote focus. No gradients on buttons, no credentials in packaged assets, no decorative processing that burdens this older television. Preserve the original source and license. State actual webOS limitations honestly. Screen off means panel power only; never put the television into standby or stop the music.

## Accessibility
Every control must work with directional keys and Enter. Provide text labels, maintain focus after navigation, and respect reduced motion. No accessibility certification has been requested or claimed.
